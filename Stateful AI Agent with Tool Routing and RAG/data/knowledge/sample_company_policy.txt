Sample Company Policy — Educational Data Only

Annual Leave
All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave already taken is subtracted from this allowance. Unused leave may carry over up to 5 days with manager approval. This sample policy is not legal or HR advice.

Order Cancellation
An order may be cancelled automatically only while its status is Processing. Shipped orders require a support review. Any cancellation action must receive explicit human approval before a record is changed.

Expense Approval
Expenses up to $500 require a line-manager approval. Expenses above $500 require both line-manager and Finance approval. The agent may explain this rule but cannot approve expenses.

Remote Work
Employees may request up to three remote-working days per week. The employee's manager decides based on team coverage and local policy.

Data Handling
Employee and customer data must be used only for an authorized business purpose. Credentials, access tokens, and sensitive personal information must never be placed in prompts, logs, or browser-visible output.
